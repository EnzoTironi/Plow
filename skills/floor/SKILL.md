# Floor

Leaves may name a principle in JSON. Zoen never speaks these names.
This file is the index. `skill_view` the matching `principle-*` **in
full** when it applies. Do not substitute this list for the leaf.

**Never Block on the Human.** Proceed. Notify with pictures and video.
Do not wait for a yes to start, prove, or open a PR. High-risk merge
waits. Stop if they say no. Do not stall for software they do not have.
